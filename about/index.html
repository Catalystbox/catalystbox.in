What is CatalystBox?

CatalystBox is an education intelligence and improvement platform. Reportcard provides institution directories and privacy-protected aggregate feedback. Marketplace connects institutional needs with relevant providers. ThinkMap offers free practical decision tools. We also work with education institutions on analytics, AI consulting, custom AI solutions and research or board partnerships.

Reportcard

Institution directories and privacy-protected aggregate feedback for schools and colleges. Benchmark publication depends on sufficient qualifying evidence; individual answers are not published.

ThinkMap tools

  • Education ROI: weigh the cost and possible returns of an education path.
  • Margin: compare a business or self-employment path with a job.
  • Podium: explore sporting-career scenarios.
  • Exam Compass: explore exam fit against subject strengths.
  • Mind Mirror: reflect on your thinking and self-image.

These tools are illustrative starting points for reflection, not forecasts, diagnoses or promises.
